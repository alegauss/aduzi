ADUZI IS INSTALLED. FIRST STEPS

1. Open it
   From the Start menu (type Aduzi), or tick the box on the next page.

2. Bring your sessions
   Coming from MobaXterm or PuTTY? Press Ctrl+Shift+I to import your saved sessions; a session type Aduzi does not support is named in the report instead of being dropped.
   Hosts in your ~/.ssh/config already appear, ProxyJump and Include included.

3. Find any action
   Ctrl+Shift+P opens the command palette. Aduzi has no menu: every action is in the palette.

4. Type into several sessions at once
   Ctrl+Shift+B.


TO REMOVE IT

Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Aduzi > Uninstall.
